Our Mission

The Challenge We Face

Rising global $CO_2$ levels are accelerating the greenhouse effect, threatening environmental stability. Simultaneously, urban air pollution—filled with particulate matter and harmful gases—poses a direct and immediate threat to public health.

Our Vision

Our vision is to integrate bio-technology seamlessly into existing urban infrastructure. We aim to create healthier, more sustainable communities by transforming everyday structures into active air purification systems, tackling both pollution and climate change at their source.

The Solution

The EcoGlow System

Our innovation, EcoGlow, is a solar-powered street lamp that actively purifies the air. It combines a multi-stage physical filter with a living algae photobioreactor to remove harmful pollutants and convert $CO_2$ into oxygen.

How It Works

1. Intake & Filtration

A fan pulls polluted air into the unit. It first passes through HEPA and activated carbon filters to remove dust, smoke, and harmful gases.

2. Bioreactor ($CO_2$ Capture)

The filtered air enters the algae chamber, where *Chlorella vulgaris* absorbs carbon dioxide and releases oxygen through photosynthesis.

3. Sustainable Power

Solar panels power the entire unit—including the fan, light, and monitoring sensors—allowing it to run sustainably and off-grid.

Technical Benefits

  • 92% $CO_2$ Capture

    High-density *Chlorella vulgaris* microalgae optimized for rapid gas–liquid exchange.

  • Stable Oxygen Output

    Up to 2.1 g $O_2$ / 100 g culture / day via optimized photon absorption.

  • Low-Energy Design

    Cuts power consumption by 55% compared to conventional photobioreactors.
